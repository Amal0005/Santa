# 🎅 ElfVault

Welcome to **ElfVault**, a magical digital platform designed to encourage, track, and reward acts of kindness. Built with the **MERN Stack** (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js), this application transforms good deeds into "Spirit Coins" and holiday cheer!

## 🌟 How It Works

The Santa Kindness Wallet gamifies the concept of being "nice" during the holiday season. Here is the user journey:

1.  **Sign Up**: Users create an account to start their journey on the "Nice List".
2.  **Do Good**: Users perform real-world acts of kindness and log them in the app.
3.  **Earn Rewards**:
    *   **Spirit Coins**: Different deeds award different amounts of coins (e.g., helping a neighbor pays more than a simple compliment).
    *   **Nice Meter**: Every good deed fills up the user's "Nice Meter", moving them from "Naughty" to "Santa Approved".
4.  **Spend & Decorate**: Coins can be spent in the **Kindness Shop** to buy decorations for a personal digital Christmas Tree.
5.  **Community Goal**: All users contribute to a global "North Pole Spirit Hub", aiming to fill the world with enough kindness to power Santa's sleigh.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎁 Personal Dashboard
*   **Nice Meter**: Visual progress bar tracking your "Naughty or Nice" status.
*   **Recent Deeds**: A quick log of your latest kind actions.
*   **Trophy Room**: Showcases unlocked badges (e.g., "Kindness Hero") and stickers earned by reaching milestones.

### 📜 Logging Deeds
*   **Add Deed**: An easy interface to select from categories of kindness or log custom deeds.
*   **Automatic Rewards**: The system automatically calculates coins and Nice Meter impact based on the deed's difficulty.

### 🎄 My Kindness Tree
*   **Interactive Decoration**: A drag-and-drop style interface where you can decorate your own tree.
*   **Shop**: Use your hard-earned Spirit Coins to buy ornaments, lights, and toppers.

### 🌍 North Pole Spirit Hub (Community)
*   **Global Impact**: Connects all users to show the total acts of kindness and coins generated worldwide.
*   **Live Counter**: Real-time updates on the global "Spirit Level".
*   **Christmas Countdown**: A live timer counting down the days, hours, and minutes until Christmas.

### 📨 Santa's Post Office
*   **Write to Santa**: Users can spend coins to send personalized letters to Santa.
*   **Santa's Reply**: Receive instant, whimsical responses from the North Pole (powered by the backend).

### 🏆 Leaderboard
*   **Top Elves**: See who is leading the charge in spreading kindness.
*   **Motivation**: Friendly competition to encourage more good deeds.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

*   **Frontend**: React (Vite), TailwindCSS, Framer Motion (for animations), Lucide React (icons).
*   **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js.
*   **Database**: MongoDB (Mongoose) for storing users, deeds, and tree states.
*   **Authentication**: JWT (JSON Web Tokens) for secure access.
